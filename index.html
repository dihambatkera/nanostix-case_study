Creativity & Innovation in Nanostix

How Creativity and Innovation Helped Solve Real Business Challenges

Nanostix is a Malaysian vape company that produces vape devices and e-liquids. The company focuses on product quality, innovation, and customer safety. As the vape industry continued to grow, Nanostix faced several business challenges including changing regulations, illegal products, safety concerns, and customer expectations. Through creativity and innovation, the company developed new ideas and improved its business operations to overcome these challenges.

Key Business Challenges

Problem 01: Undang-undang Berkaitan Vape Perlu Diselaraskan – Nanostix

Introduction: Different states in Malaysia enforce varying vape regulations, creating legal ambiguity and operational confusion for legitimate vape companies.

"Vape laws in Malaysia need urgent standardization across all states to eliminate confusion and protect legal industry stakeholders."

How The Problem Was Identified

Nanostix observed that inconsistent local council and state-level directives created business uncertainty across different regions in Malaysia.

Creative & Innovative Solution

Nanostix advocated for a single nationwide legal framework, partnering with industry chambers to push for standardized federal regulations.

Key Benefits Achieved

  • Standardized national regulatory compliance
  • Clear operational guidelines for distributors
  • Increased investment and market stability
  • Protection of legitimate businesses against ambiguity

Problem 02: Sustained Crackdown Needed to Dismantle Underground Vape

Introduction: Illegal vape products and underground syndicates endanger consumer safety and bypass health standards.

Problem

Unregulated counterfeit devices entered the market without safety certifications or lab testing.

How The Problem Was Identified

Intelligence investigations and law enforcement reports revealed illicit products distributed through unvetted channels.

Creative & Innovative Solution

Nanostix urged sustained intelligence-led government enforcement while integrating secure QR-code authentication on all packaging.

Key Benefits Achieved

  • Guaranteed authentic product safety for users
  • Dismantling of illegal counterfeit networks
  • Strengthened consumer trust and brand integrity
  • Alignment with law enforcement initiatives

Problem 05: Excise Duty on Vape Liquids The Way Forward

Introduction: Before formal taxation, unregulated vape liquids entered the market without safety oversight or tax contributions. Industry leaders highlighted excise duty as the crucial way forward to regulate the sector.

Problem

Absence of a structured excise duty framework allowed uncertified e-liquids to circulate without government tax compliance or lab safety testing.

How The Problem Was Identified

Industry reports from Free Malaysia Today demonstrated that formal excise duty implementation would separate legitimate tax-compliant manufacturers from illicit traders.

Creative & Innovative Solution

Nanostix actively supported government excise duty initiatives, upgrading production facilities to meet official tax banderole and SIRIM safety requirements.

Key Benefits Achieved

  • Fair government taxation revenue generation
  • Formal industry legalization and standard oversight
  • Strict lab-tested e-liquid quality compliance
  • Elimination of untaxed contraband vape liquids
